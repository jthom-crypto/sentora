Institutional DeFi Operations Desk

A simulated Sentora operations desk supporting an institutional client across three Sentora-curated Morpho vaults.

  • Client: Holds assets in its own Fireblocks custody, requests transactions, and signs.
  • Operations: Prepares transactions, tracks execution, reconciles positions, and reports.
  • Vault data: Sourced from Morpho's public API.

Client activity, custody approvals, and internal records are simulated. Values are labeled observed, estimated, or simulated.

    Funding Forecast Simulated obligations

    Cash carried forward by date and asset: opening cash + inflows − outflows = closing cash. Settled cash counts only confirmed balances and can go negative when obligations exceed it. Projected cash adds signed or broadcast withdrawals and any planned vault draws, none of which have settled. When projected cash runs short, the forecast plans a draw on standard vault liquidity, and each draw reduces what is left for later dates.
