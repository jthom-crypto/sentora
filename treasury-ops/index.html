DeFi Treasury Operations

A simulated institutional treasury workflow for allocating PYUSD to Sentora-curated vaults.

  • Client: Holds PYUSD in its own Fireblocks custody, with upcoming payments and a required cash reserve.
  • Operations: Determines how much can be invested, compares eligible vaults, checks policy limits, and prepares the allocation for approval.
  • Vault data: Four vaults accepting PYUSD, sourced from Morpho’s public API.

Client balances, payments, policy limits, and approvals are simulated. Values are labeled observed, estimated, or simulated.

Vault Comparison Observed

Sentora-curated PYUSD vaults on Ethereum. Eligibility uses the treasury's vault criteria; a higher APY alone does not make a vault suitable.

Vault criteria: PYUSD on Ethereum; APY excluding rewards at least 2.00%; total assets at least $20M; performance fee at most 20%; standard withdrawal liquidity observed. APYs are Morpho net APYs, already after management and performance fees, so fees are not deducted again from income.
