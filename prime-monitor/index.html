PRIME Market Operations Monitor

A simulated operations workflow for Sentora's PRIME Main vault on Morpho, which lends PYUSD against PRIME, a token backed by Figure home equity (HELOC) loans.

  • Vault and market: allocations, withdrawal liquidity, utilization, and borrower positions from Morpho's public API.
  • Operations: tracks exposure and liquidity, runs data checks, and coordinates responses through risk approval, execution, and verification.
  • Case study: one simulated borrower position, monitored and unwound the way a repo desk manages a financed MBS position.

Watch levels, stress scenarios, actions, approvals, and the case-study position are simulated. Values are labeled observed, estimated, or simulated.

    PRIME Background
    • Exposure: HELOC-backed financing through Figure.
    • Structure: financing between loan origination and sale or securitization.
    • Risks: credit deterioration, delayed securitization, limited exit liquidity, and counterparty or technical failures.

    Facility terms

    • Repo facility with Figure Lending as repo seller; maximum 98% LTV with automated paydown if collateral coverage drops.
    • Loans sit in the warehouse about 42 days on average before securitization; loans 60+ days delinquent are removed from the pool.
    • Token management fee 0.50%; issued on Ethereum and Solana.

    Borrower statistics

    • 2024 originations: average FICO 749, average income about $187,000.
